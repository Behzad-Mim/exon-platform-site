Exon Platform

A ledger that works with the internet off.

Exon Platform keeps the books for a hawala and currency-exchange business — transfers, cash in and out, exchanges, customer accounts and daily balance sheets. Everything is stored on your own device, encrypted with your own password.

دفتری که بدون اینترنت کار می‌کند.

اکسون پلتفرم حساب‌های یک صرافی و حواله را نگه می‌دارد — ارسال و دریافت حواله، رسید و برد نقد، تبادله، حساب مشتریان و بیلانس روزانه. همه‌چیز روی دستگاه خودتان می‌ماند و با رمز خودتان رمزگذاری می‌شود.

What it does

Offline first

No account to create, no server to reach. The app opens and works with the network off — which is the point, not a fallback.

Encrypted on the device

The book is a SQLCipher database locked with a key derived from your password. A copied file is ciphertext, not a customer list.

Several currencies in one deal

Afghani and dollars in the same transfer, each side of the account moving correctly, and every currency inside a document netting to zero.

Three languages

Persian, Pashto and English, with the Hijri Shamsi calendar and Persian digits where they belong.

Windows and Android

The same ledger on a desktop and a phone, able to sync directly to each other over the local network without going through anybody's server.

Your backups, yours

Backups stay on the device, and optionally in a folder of your own Google Drive — encrypted before they leave.

About the Google connection

Connecting Google is optional and off until you turn it on. When you do, the app asks only for permission to see and manage the files it creates itself in your Drive — never the rest of it. What it uploads is already encrypted with your book password, so the copy in your Drive cannot be read by Google, by us, or by anyone who does not have that password. That also means that if you forget it, the cloud copy cannot be opened either.

دربارهٔ اتصال گوگل

اتصال گوگل اختیاری است و تا خودتان روشنش نکنید خاموش می‌ماند. وقتی روشن کنید، اپ فقط اجازهٔ دیدن و مدیریت فایل‌هایی را می‌خواهد که خودش می‌سازد — نه بقیهٔ درایو شما. آنچه بالا می‌رود از پیش با رمز دفترتان رمز شده، پس نسخهٔ داخل درایو نه برای گوگل خواناست، نه برای ما، نه برای کسی که آن رمز را ندارد. و همین یعنی اگر رمز را فراموش کنید، نسخهٔ ابری هم باز نمی‌شود.
